About Trivia Night

A single-device host app for trivia night: the TV shows the board, and the host runs everything from one screen.

  • Setup: name your teams — fun names are suggested automatically, so just edit, add, or remove — and pick a mode.
  • Jeopardy: click a point cell to open the question. Click a team to mark them green (correct) or red (wrong) — click a marked team again to change or clear it. Reveal shows the answer; Done banks the points.
  • Trivia: click a category name to start that round — the point cells are not clickable. Mark a team green when they answer; click again to clear. Reveal shows the answer; Next moves on.
  • On the TV: easiest is to mirror the Mac's screen (everything works, including Load folder and your real clips). The TV's own browser can open the live link (shipped board only) — test it before the night. A saved game belongs to the address it was played on, so always open the same one.
  • No mouse? A TV remote's D-pad (or Tab / Shift-Tab) moves between cells, categories and buttons; OK (Enter / Space) presses.
  • Oops? "Back to board" (or browser Back) drops that question's marks and keeps the cell and scores. The game saves as you play: after a reload, press Resume game (folder board: Load folder first, same folder). Reloaded? Press Resume game, not Start.
  • Mid-game: on Setup, renames and mode switches keep the game; adding or removing a team starts fresh. That and Reset game (clears scores and played cells) ask first; Back to board drops Setup edits.
  • Your own games: the shipped board is one text file — questions.yaml. Load folder plays your own folder (questions.yaml + audio/ + images/) and asks first mid-game; Create new scaffolds one (Chrome or Edge): pick or make a folder inside Documents, not Documents itself, and allow editing when Chrome asks. Team names carry over. Problems are listed by round and question, with typo hints; "Back to setup" keeps your game.

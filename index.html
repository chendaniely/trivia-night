About Trivia Night

A single-device host app for trivia night: the TV shows the board, and the host runs everything from one screen.

  • Setup: name your teams — fun names are suggested automatically, so just edit, add, or remove — and pick a mode.
  • Jeopardy: click a dollar cell to open the question. Click a team to mark them green (correct) or red (wrong) — click a marked team again to change or clear it. Reveal shows the answer; Done banks the points.
  • Trivia: click a category name to start that round — the dollar cells are not clickable. Mark a team green when they answer; click again to clear. Next moves on.
  • Mistapped? Every question screen has a "Back to board" button that keeps everything.
  • Mid-game: the Setup button returns here. Same team count resumes the game (rename in place); a different count starts a fresh one.
  • Your own games: the shipped board is one text file — questions.yaml. Or use Load folder to play a board from your own folder (questions.yaml + audio/ + images/); Create new scaffolds one (Chrome or Edge).

Source & docs: github.com/chendaniely/trivia-night

About Trivia Night

A single-device host app for trivia night: the TV shows the board, and the host runs everything from one screen.

  • Setup: name your teams — fun names are suggested automatically, so just edit, add, or remove — and pick a mode.
  • Jeopardy: click a dollar cell to open the question. Click a team to mark them green (correct) or red (wrong) — click a marked team again to change or clear it. Reveal shows the answer; Done banks the points.
  • Trivia: click a category name to start that round — the dollar cells are not clickable. Mark a team green when they answer; click again to clear. Reveal shows the answer; Next moves on.
  • Oops? "Back to board" (or the browser's Back) keeps everything, and the game saves as you play: after a reload, press Resume game.
  • Mid-game: on Setup, renames and mode switches keep the game; adding or removing a team starts fresh. That and Reset game ask first.
  • Your own games: the shipped board is one text file — questions.yaml. Load folder plays your own folder (questions.yaml + audio/ + images/) and asks first mid-game; Create new scaffolds one (Chrome or Edge). Team names carry over, and if a folder has a problem, "Back to setup" keeps your game.
